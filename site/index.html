AI models that run on your own PC.

Foldmate runs AI models on your own PC. You don't need an account, and your chats stay on your computer unless you choose a cloud model.

Download for Windows See what it can do

Windows 11, 64-bit, with a Mac preview for Apple silicon. The latest version is 1.1.17.

Private by default

Your chats are encrypted on your disk

The key is protected by your Windows account.

No telemetry or analytics

Foldmate doesn't report what you do, and it never sends a support report for you.

Models run on this PC

A cloud model is only used when you pick one, and Foldmate asks before it sends an existing chat.

Web research goes direct by default

Turn on Through Tor for the private route. It never falls back to direct.

You decide what it may do

Each chat has an approval mode: Ask every time, Approve for me, or Full access.

What goes online, and when

What it can do

Research the web

A bot searches and reads pages for you, directly or Through Tor.

Use your desktop for you

Computer use and PC commands, with an approval card before actions on your PC when you want one.

A chat bar at a keypress

Press Alt+` (the key under Esc) to open the chat bar.

Your phone (beta)

A paired iPhone can use Foldmate on this PC through Tor with no account, or over your own Wi-Fi.

Connected services

Gmail, Outlook and Microsoft 365, MCP servers and more.

Coding with OpenCode

OpenCode works inside its project's sealed box.

All features

New in 1.1.17

Smarter computer use: the assistant now uses an exact calculator for totals with tax or delivery, price comparisons and free calendar times, and it touches nothing on screen, so it asks for no approval card. In our tests with the 27B model, pass rates were the same or better and tasks took about 11% less time on average.
