# Getting help with Foldmate

## Look here first

- **Foldmate's Help:** press **F1** in Foldmate (or open **Settings & tools › Support › Help**). It works offline.
- **Troubleshooting and FAQ:** the [README](README.md#troubleshooting-and-faq) answers common questions about the
  SmartScreen warning, web research and virtualization, updates, the Mac preview and Through Tor.
- **What you need:** the [system requirements](README.md#what-you-need) list the Windows version, RAM and disk space.

## Ask a question or report a problem

Open an issue at https://github.com/griffin300/foldmate-releases/issues/new/choose and choose one:

- **Bug report:** something doesn't work the way it should.
- **Feature request:** an idea for something new, or a change.
- **Help with setup or models:** a question about installing, web research, the phone app or choosing a model.

Corrections to the translations are welcome there too.

## What to include

- Your **Foldmate version**.
- Your **Windows version** (or, for the Mac preview, your macOS version and Mac model).
- Your **graphics card**, or "none", and how much **RAM** your PC has.
- The **model** you were using. For a cloud model, the provider too.
- **What happened**, and what you expected instead.
- The **steps** that make it happen again.
- Whether it happened **in the phone app**, and how the phone was connected (Tor, Same Wi-Fi or Tailscale).
- A **support report**, if you can: **Settings & tools › Support › Support report** shows a short summary of your PC
  and Foldmate. Read it, save it, and attach it yourself. Foldmate never sends it for you.

## What not to include

Issues are public. Please don't paste chats, keys or passwords. A support report already leaves them out.

## Security problems

Please don't report security problems in a public issue. Read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
